Building a nightlife platform for bars and clubs

My own startup project: a platform that brings bars and clubs together with their guests, taken from the first idea to a working demo app.

Role
Founder: idea, business model, product concept
Status
Demo app finished, pilot planned for 2027
Tools
Market research, product design, AI coding agent, React Native

Idea

A night out still involves a lot of guesswork, because you rarely know in advance where things are actually happening. At the same time, bars and clubs have few good ways to reach guests on the evenings that matter to them. The platform connects both sides.

What I did

I developed the business model, analysed the market and competitors and wrote a detailed product concept with a design for every screen. Based on these specifications, an AI coding agent built a working demo app under my direction, which I reviewed and accepted step by step. The demo runs on real venues in Munich with simulated guests.

Status

The demo is finished. Next, I am testing the core assumptions with venue operators ahead of a pilot planned for 2027. The business model is not public yet, but I am happy to walk you through the demo in person.
