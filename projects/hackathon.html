Organising Zeppelin University's first hackathon

24 hours, around 110 participants and the first event of its kind at Zeppelin University, organised by the ZU AI Club.

Role
Organising team, ZU AI Club
Date
February 2027
Responsible for
Sponsoring, catering, coordination with partners

Idea

Zeppelin University has never hosted a hackathon. We want to bring students from different programmes together for 24 hours to turn ideas into working prototypes.

Preparation

Organising an event for around 110 people means finding sponsors, arranging catering for a full day and night and coordinating with local partners. The event platform is already set up.

Status

The hackathon is currently in preparation and will take place in February 2027. Results and impressions will follow after the event.
