Immovio: Finding new-build projects automatically

An AI-powered competitor monitor for residential developers: Immovio reads developers’ own project pages and turns them into unit-level prices, availability and sales progress.

Role
Concept and build, on my own
Origin
Grew out of my summer internship at Phoenix Living
Tools
Python, Flask, SQLite, Playwright, OpenAI API, Excel export

Problem

Anyone in new-build sales who wants to know what the competition is planning has to click through dozens of property developer websites. Tracking prices, completion dates and new projects by hand takes a lot of time, and changes often go unnoticed for too long.

Solution

Immovio searches developer websites automatically and uses AI to read out the project data, from the specification down to the price of individual apartments. This brings the entire market analysis into a single application and gives a clear overview of what is currently on offer in a city. Results can be explored in a dashboard, on a map and in a detailed Excel export. Newly discovered competitors are only added after manual approval.

Result

The database now covers more than 800 projects with over 3,000 individual apartments from more than 200 developers. Measured against an independent sample, the tool captures about two thirds of the available information. Immovio is still in active development, and the biggest remaining gap is apartment listings hidden in embedded widgets.
