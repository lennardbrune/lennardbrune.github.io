Humanoid robots in industry

Between automation, legitimacy and responsibility: a qualitative study on how humanoid robots change the way stakeholders see the companies that use them.

Type
Research project in a team of three, Zeppelin University
Supervisor
Prof. Dr. Matthias Weiß
Methods
Qualitative interviews, analysis in MAXQDA
Grade
1.0, the top German grade

Question

Humanoid robots are moving from research labs into factories. We asked how introducing them affects the legitimacy a company has in the eyes of its stakeholders, from employees to the public.

Approach

We conducted eleven qualitative interviews with manufacturing companies, a robot manufacturer, a trade union and an industry expert, and analysed them systematically in MAXQDA.

Findings

Perceived benefits alone do not explain whether companies adopt humanoid robots. What matters is acceptance among the workforce, early involvement of works councils, and safety and regulation.
